**Result-shaping logic**

```sql
-- result-shaping view: kpi_001_results
SELECT
  date_trunc('month', CAST("ServiceDate" AS DATE)) AS month,
  "LineOfBusiness" AS lineofbusiness,
  "PayorID" AS payorid,
  "Gender" AS gender,
  date_diff('year', CAST("DOB" AS DATE), CAST("ServiceDate" AS DATE)) AS age,
  ROUND(SUM("PaidAmount"), 2) AS sum_paidamount
FROM "kpi_001_features"
WHERE "LineOfBusiness" = 'Medicare' AND date_diff('year', CAST("DOB" AS DATE), CAST("ServiceDate" AS DATE)) > 50
GROUP BY date_trunc('month', CAST("ServiceDate" AS DATE)), "LineOfBusiness", "PayorID", "Gender", date_diff('year', CAST("DOB" AS DATE), CAST("ServiceDate" AS DATE))
ORDER BY date_trunc('month', CAST("ServiceDate" AS DATE)), "LineOfBusiness", "PayorID", "Gender", date_diff('year', CAST("DOB" AS DATE), CAST("ServiceDate" AS DATE))
```
